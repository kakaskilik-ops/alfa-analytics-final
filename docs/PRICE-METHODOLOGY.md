# ALFA price evidence and assessment methodology

This release targets **visible numeric observations for all 13 materials**, not 13 invented spot quotations. Data types remain separate: World Bank monthly references; public buyer indications; supplier catalogue quotations; and undated **from** prices. None is silently inserted into regional spot indices. The production release is authorised by the owner's 17 November task; this document describes the implementation before final live verification.

## Selection and current factual observations

Prefer an available verified regional quotation with its specification, then the official World Bank monthly reference for the four supported metals. Other materials use the latest explicitly attributed supplier observation. Older catalogue forms remain separate alternatives in the detail dialog. Sorting is by the displayed number, explicitly across differing data types, not a comparable market index.

These are verified **published facts**, not a declaration that supplier numbers are the prevailing market level. Rare Metals bids are not completed sales or firm executable offers. The two ferroalloy floors may be old and must be confirmed with the supplier. Their unknown age is always visible. No average or consensus is invented because available supplier forms, purity, dates, quantities and delivery terms are not independently comparable.

Mass conversion only: 1 metric tonne = 1,000 kg; 1 avoirdupois pound = **0.45359237 kg**. USD/kg = USD/lb ÷ 0.45359237; USD/t = USD/kg × 1,000. Currency and geography are not manufactured by conversion. Rare Metals itself converts EUR/kg into the USD/lb it publishes; ALFA preserves that published original and does not independently replace its FX assumption. A price per kg of Mo/W content is never treated as per kg of alloy.

QS Advanced Materials' 2023 catalogue additionally lists Plate/Sheet >10mm at Ta $580/lb, Nb $170/lb and Zr grade 702 $80/lb. Those three old, differently specified US supplier observations are alternatives, not replacements for the current bid snapshots and not equivalent to pure-metal spot quotations. A year-only date is internally anchored on January 1 for storage, but the UI shows **2023 (catalogue year)**, never a fabricated precise publication date.

## Sources, rights, access and automation

* [World Bank Pink Sheet](https://www.worldbank.org/en/research/commodity-markets): existing official workbook adapter, dated monthly history and [terms](https://data.worldbank.org/summary-terms-of-use). Current Supabase rows were read directly; four cards use their actual latest values, not new hardcoded frontend prices. Existing daily scheduled collector is preserved.
* [Rare Metals public bids](https://metal-supply.biz/): seven numeric material/purity rows, USD/lb excluding VAT. [Legal notice](https://metal-supply.biz/impressum) identifies a German operator; the US telephone number does **not** make these US prices. The registered address defines the supplier region, not delivery destination. [robots.txt](https://metal-supply.biz/robots.txt) permits this price page; search paths are excluded and not fetched. Only minimal factual quotations are publicly displayed with source links, no copyrighted articles/images/database reproduction. This is not a claim of a blanket open-data licence. The contact form/security mechanism is not used or bypassed.
* [Metotechnics ferroalloy list](https://metotechnics.com/ferrosplavy-cena.htm): two factual lower bounds from the supplier's public English price list. Its disclaimer explicitly makes prices informational, not a legally binding offer. [Contact page](https://metotechnics.com/contact.htm) identifies the Moscow office and states office and warehouse differ. No shipping location is inferred. The Russian `metotech.ru` host returned 403 and was not bypassed; the separately published English domain returned 200 without authentication or modified identity. No explicit price publication date is available.
* [QS public catalogue](https://www.qsrarematerials.com/uploadfile/down/20230303/QS%20Product%20Cata.pdf): three factual product-price references with links to the supplier. [Terms](https://www.qsrarematerials.com/term-of-use-a-25.html) permit use for purchasing/promoting its products without competing with it. The platform does not sell products or republish the catalogue. The [supplier website](https://www.qsrarematerials.com/) identifies Troy, Michigan. Original PDF SHA-256 `685c9f10554296ae0ab1bdde2f7b38a0002b6e25733fcc09439b15c1a4f6b7d6` is pinned: a changed file is withheld for review, not parsed using old constants.

The new protected `collect-price-evidence` checks the three supplier sources daily; it has no AI dependency, no public write route and no extra key. Its private lease uses the existing collector-token mechanism. A source failure records a sanitized error, preserves the last valid observations and allows other sources to continue. Repeated same-day values deduplicate; changed values retain separate historical rows. Source health is in the existing source registry. The original/private evidence payload is not publicly readable. The PDF is stored only privately; public UI exposes selected numeric facts and original links.

`ops/activate-price-evidence.sql` guards the exact project `vlfzjvwgrvyllvshwmzr`, enables the new daily job and creates only `alfa-price-evidence-daily`. Existing schedules, Groq, DNS, SSL and other projects are unchanged. One request per supplier daily, 15-second network timeout, bounded HTML/PDF sizes, strict host allowlists/no arbitrary redirects, 1MiB existing private payload limit. No paid service or anti-bot bypass is involved. Hosted fetch success must be checked during release; local HTTP 200 alone is not called an operational cloud collector.

## Research beyond the connected sources

All 13 materials and three regions were investigated. USGS [MCS 2026 release](https://www.usgs.gov/data/mineral-commodity-summaries-2026-data-release) is explicitly CC0; however PDF/CSV/ScienceBase endpoints returned 403 here. It was not activated from search snippets. USGS Mo oxide, W concentrate, ferroniobium and tantalum oxide content values must not substitute for pure metals or the requested alloys. LME/CME/SHFE access and redistribution cannot be assumed free; none was added. Asian Metal/SMM require login/licensing for the relevant series; they were not bypassed. Foundry Trade Journal, WMcarbide and CTIA requests failed; their indexed snippets were not imported. Normet's source domain returned a hosting placeholder, despite search snippets containing price tables, so it was rejected. SDI's public surcharge letter was read, but its underlying CRU-derived index and surcharge are not a direct allocated physical FeMo purchase offer. RMC Polska's public PLN bids were inspected but not used to fabricate extra independently comparable observations or regions.

Potential observed supplier-region positions: Europe 9 (7 pure-metal bids + FeMo/FeW floors), US 3 (old catalogue forms), Asia 0 = **12/39 supplier-region positions** if all three source imports pass verification. Current independently confirmed regional spot quotations remain **0/39**. Four global World Bank references are not regional positions. Counts of evidence, firm transactions and quotations must always be labelled separately.

## What was learned from Argus, and what ALFA can actually calculate

Argus' public [methodology overview](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/methodology) and [Non-Ferrous Markets description](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/solutions/products/argus-non-ferrous-markets) explain its use of market participants, verified transactions, bids/offers and analyst review under product-specific specifications. This cannot be reproduced by scraping prices and taking a majority. The [published guide](https://www.argusmedia.com/-/media/project/argusmedia/mainsite/english/documents-and-files/methodology/argus-non-ferrous-markets.pdf) specifies grade, form, location, delivery/tax basis, lot sizes and timing. No Argus price data was acquired or redistributed, no Argus branding or affiliation is claimed.

`shared/priceAssessment.ts` implements independent **ALFA-PRA-1** safeguards. Grade/form/country/region/delivery/tax/mass basis, lot range and observation window must match. A transaction must have a documented verification ID and bilateral verification; public price lists never satisfy that condition. Duplicate documents and inconsistent revisions cannot multiply evidence. ALFA's own deliberately conservative policy requires three independently contributed qualifying transactions, flags a contributor above 50% of volume, and defers a highest/lowest spread above 25% for review. Those are **ALFA review thresholds**, not claimed Argus rules or validated accuracy/confidence percentages. Qualifying transactions receive a transparent volume-weighted calculation with low/high, volume, included IDs and exclusion reasons; otherwise price is null.

This library has synthetic tests for future verified-transaction workflows; it is **not a live Argus-equivalent market assessment service**. No such transaction set has been collected in this release and no synthetic test trade is saved in production. The current UI honestly states that independent confirmation under identical terms is absent for public supplier observations. A production assessment network still requires permitted bilateral deal submissions, counterparty verification and documented analyst review, or licensed data. The existing World Bank statistical analytics remain available separately.

## Tests and release verification

Captured fixtures contain minimal real source rows, public Supabase World Bank observations and provenance hashes, not demonstration prices. `test:prices` inserts the 12 actual supplier observations in isolated PGlite, validates independent SQL/JS unit conversions, deduplication, source/unit constraints and role permissions. Anonymous users cannot access payload IDs, raw evidence, collectors or writes. The browser test combines those test-database reads with actual captured World Bank rows to verify 13 numeric cards, ferroalloy separation, lower-bound warnings, monthly source/date/unit display, details and mobile layout. Real production checks after release must independently confirm all 13 rendered values and actual source/cron health.
